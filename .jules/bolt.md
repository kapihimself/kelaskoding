## 2025-05-15 - IDE Rendering Optimization
**Learning:** In the 3-pane IDE layout, the `MaterialPanel` and `AITutor` components were re-rendering on every keystroke in the `EditorPanel` because they are siblings sharing the same parent state (`code`) in `Workspace.tsx`, even though they don't consume that state. Additionally, the `PreviewPanel` was causing minor lag because it was re-rendering synchronously with every keystroke.
**Action:** Applied `React.memo` to `MaterialPanel`, `AITutor`, and `PreviewPanel` to eliminate unnecessary re-renders. Implemented `useDeferredValue` for the `code` prop passed to `PreviewPanel` and removed the manual `setTimeout` debounce to leverage React's built-in transition prioritization, ensuring the editor remains responsive during heavy typing.
